📊 Explanation of Charts

Price Trend with 7-Day Moving Average

Blue line: Daily stock closing prices

Orange line: 7-day moving average → smooths short-term ups and downs

Usefulness: Helps identify long-term price trends

Daily Return %

Bars showing percentage gain/loss each day

Above 0: Stock gained that day

Below 0: Stock lost that day

Usefulness: Helps spot daily volatility and sudden jumps/drops

Rolling Volatility (7-Day Std Dev)

Measures how much the price fluctuates over 7 days

Higher value = more risk

Lower value = stable price movement

Usefulness: Useful for risk assessment and trading decisions

Cumulative Return Over Time

Shows the total return since the start of the data

Always accumulates gains/losses across days

Usefulness: Helps track overall investment performance
